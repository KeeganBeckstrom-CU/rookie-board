Age is age on draft day (April 22, 2027), shown only where a birth date was found. Player art is a generic silhouette in each school's colors, and school crests are original seals, not official logos. Stats come from ESPN, school athletics sites and college stat trackers. Position ranks blend recent big boards and dynasty rookie rankings and refresh weekly; the Top 35 blends the superflex market consensus (60%) with our board (40%) and is grouped into tiers. Letter grades and the 0–10 production score compare each player with the other prospects at his position on this board, using 2026 stats and the market data on his card; they are not scouting grades. Value/Pricey tags compare our board with the superflex market (6+ spots apart). Injury tags come from the newest injury note in the weekly updates and show its date. Team needs are a youth count only (age, not talent). Favorites are saved in this browser.
